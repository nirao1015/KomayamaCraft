KomayamaCraft Terrain Tiles 6x8

- Tile size: 1920 x 1080 pixels
- Grid: 6 columns x 8 rows
- Naming: terrain_1A.png through terrain_6H.png
- Horizontal index: 1 to 6 from left to right
- Vertical index: A to H from top to bottom
- Ocean areas outside the coastline use PNG alpha transparency.
- Terrain, vegetation, small habitat details, and construction clearings are included.
- Creatures, the spaceship, the dimensional rift, and major resource objects are intentionally omitted for later placement in Unity.

Preview files:
- continent_tiles_preview.png: terrain placed over an ocean texture
- continent_tiles_preview_transparent.png: terrain with its actual alpha background
